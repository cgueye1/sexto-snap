Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Seddo ("Seddo", "we", "us", or "our") is a tourism and events companion application for Senegal, published by Innov & Impact Africa. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Seddo mobile application (the "App"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Contact information (such as your name or email address) only if you choose to contact us or create an account, where this feature is available.

1.2 Camera and photos — the AR Hat feature

Seddo includes one augmented reality feature that lets you take photos and videos of yourself wearing a virtual traditional Senegalese hat. To provide this feature:

  • The App accesses your device's camera (and microphone, if you record a video) only while you are using the AR feature, and only after you grant permission.
  • Camera frames are processed in real time to detect the position of your head so the virtual hat can be displayed. We do not use this processing to identify you, and Seddo does not create or store any facial recognition data or biometric identifiers.
  • Photos and videos you capture are saved on your own device. They are not uploaded to our servers. You decide whether to keep, delete, or share them.
Third-party technology: the AR Hat feature is powered by Camera Kit, a technology provided by Snap Inc. When you use this feature, Snap Inc. may process certain technical and usage data as an independent service provider, as described in Snap Inc.'s Privacy Policy. Before first use of the AR feature, you will be asked to review and accept the applicable Camera Kit terms.

1.3 Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: device model, operating system version, app version, language, crash reports, and general usage statistics that help us keep the App reliable.
  • Approximate location (only if you grant permission): used to show you nearby destinations, events, and points of interest in Senegal. You can use the App without granting location access.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App's features, including tourism and event information and the AR Hat experience;
  • To maintain, improve, and secure the App, including diagnosing crashes and technical issues;
  • To respond to your questions and support requests;
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your photos, videos, or camera data for advertising.

3. Sharing of Information

We share information only with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the App (such as hosting, analytics, and crash-reporting providers, and Snap Inc. for the Camera Kit feature described above), under obligations to protect your data;
  • Authorities, where required by applicable law.

4. Data Retention

Photos and videos captured with the AR feature remain on your device under your control. Technical and usage data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • You can withdraw camera, microphone, or location permissions at any time in your device settings; the related features will simply stop working.
  • Depending on your country of residence — including under Senegal's Data Protection Act (Law No. 2008-12) and, where applicable, the EU GDPR — you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, or to object to certain processing.
  • To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we handle. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when it was last revised. Significant changes will be communicated within the App.
